System Profile

Maryam Rezaee at Your Service :)

Firstly, feel free to call me Mary, Mer, or anything that rolls better off your tongue.

But who is Mer? Well, I’m first and foremost a researcher. I mostly spend my days navigating the wonderfully chaotic fields of LLM Interpretability and Cognitively Inspired AI, as I aim to tame the neural black box by weaving human-like structure into the fabric of these systems—hoping against hope to make AI a more transparent tool that also better emulates our complex minds.

Going back, this journey of mine began with theoretical narrative algorithms and any and all attempts at structuralising human thought. However, as LLMs took off, I ended up winding my way into concept-based interpretability to tackle a problem I found deeply concerning. And these days, you’ll find me tinkering with Neurosymbolic architectures to piece together systems capable of genuine reasoning. So perhaps the best way to describe my drive would be a vision of democratic and accessible AI that is verifiable, explainable, and deeply aligned with the way we think.

I must add, I do have a full life outside of academia as well. Writing, especially poetically and creatively, is my soul, and music is its language. If I’m not waist-deep inside a random book or the rabbit hole of new articles, I’m probably obsessing over some design trying to reach aesthetic perfection to scratch an itch, or snapping endless photos of moments that tell stories.

If you’re interested, don’t hesitate to reach out. I never say no to a new project with potential!
